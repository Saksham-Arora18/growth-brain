# Weekly strategy

- **Interactive Learning** (30%): Boost repeat visits and community participation through hands‑on challenges and mini‑projects.
- **Authority Shorts** (25%): Position the creator as a trusted, responsible AI educator with quick‑hit myth‑busting content.
- **Comparative Content** (20%): Drive high watch‑time and debate by directly comparing popular AI tools.
- **Career Growth** (25%): Attract high‑intent followers seeking concrete AI career pathways and resources.

## Plan
- Monday: [Interactive Learning] AI Prompt Engineering Challenges (Weekly Reel series + Instagram Story poll for user‑submitted prompts, with a carousel recap of best solutions.)
- Tuesday: [Authority Shorts] Myth‑Busting AI Ethics in 60 Seconds (Reel series using text overlay, quick voice‑over, and a CTA to comment personal concerns; pin the series to profile highlights.)
- Wednesday: [Comparative Content] Tool‑Comparison Battles (e.g., ChatGPT vs. Claude vs. Gemini) (Carousel with side‑by‑side screenshots + short Reel demo of each tool on the same task, ending with a poll sticker to vote for the winner.)
- Thursday: [Interactive Learning] Behind‑the‑Scenes of Building a Mini AI Project (IGTV/Live split into 3‑part mini‑course, supplemented by carousel slides with code snippets and a downloadable PDF link in bio.)
- Friday: [Career Growth] AI‑Powered Career Path Maps (Infographic carousel outlining skill milestones, paired with a Reel walkthrough and a downloadable Excel template linked via Linktree.)
- Saturday: [Authority Shorts] Myth‑Busting AI Ethics in 60 Seconds (Re‑post the week’s top myth‑busting Reel as a Story highlight with a Q&A sticker to deepen engagement.)
- Sunday: [Comparative Content] Tool‑Comparison Battles (e.g., ChatGPT vs. Claude vs. Gemini) (Live Q&A recap discussing audience poll results and answering tool‑specific questions.)
